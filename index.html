New in Intellecta Plus

Read it. Hear it. Chapter by chapter.

Chapter-wise audio, mapped to the exact page of the book — so students can read, listen, or do both.

  • 50 / 50 reading view

    Book on the left, chapter list and player on the right. Hide the audio any time for full-width reading.

  • Jump to any chapter

    Click a chapter — it plays and the book turns to its start page. The next chapter follows automatically.

  • Bulk upload for librarians

    Upload a folder of chapter files in one step, link it to a book, and set each chapter’s page from an Excel sheet or on screen.

  • Principal login

    Reads and views every report and Excel export — without access to book or account management.
